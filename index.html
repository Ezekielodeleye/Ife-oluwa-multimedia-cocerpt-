A LITTLE ABOUT US

Ife Oluwa Multimedia Concept is a creative multimedia company founded and led by CEO Joseph Dosumu. We are proudly affiliated with the Catholic Creator Communication Crew, Catholic Diocese Of Oyo.

From birthdays, weddings and burials to corporate events, church programs, Catholic events, brand promotions and personal shoots, we deliver professional, affordable and timely services.
